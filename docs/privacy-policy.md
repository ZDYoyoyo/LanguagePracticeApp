## 繁體中文

### 1. 我們的原則
「英語練習」（以下稱「本 App」）採「離線優先」設計：**開發者沒有架設任何伺服器或資料庫，不會收到、也看不到你的學習資料。**

### 2. 儲存在你手機上的資料
以下資料只儲存在你的裝置上（App 的私有儲存空間），用於提供 App 功能：
- 作答紀錄（題目編號、對錯、作答時間、作答模式）
- 錯題本（錯誤次數、最後一次的錯誤答案）
- 間隔重複複習排程（下次複習日期等）
- 每日打卡紀錄
- 提示次數、提醒時間等設定
- 若你登入 Google：你的 Google 帳號 email（僅用於在設定頁顯示「已登入：xxx」）
- 從網路下載的公開題庫檔案與開發者公告（不含個人資料）

本 App 已關閉 Android 系統的自動雲端備份；**移除 App 時，上述資料會一併從手機刪除。**

### 3. Google 登入與 Google Drive（選擇性）
- 登入是**選擇性**的，不登入也能使用全部學習功能，但資料無法跨裝置保留。
- 登入後，本 App 只要求 Google Drive 的 **`drive.appdata`** 權限：只能存取 Google Drive 中「本 App 專用的隱藏資料夾」，**無法讀取、修改或刪除你 Drive 裡的任何其他檔案**。
- 用途：把第 2 點的學習資料（不含 email）備份成一個檔案，存放在**你自己的** Google Drive，以便換手機時還原。檔案佔用你的 Drive 空間，傳輸過程使用 HTTPS 加密，**不經過開發者的伺服器**。
- Google 登入與 Drive 服務由 Google 提供，適用 [Google 隱私權政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)。

### 4. 廣告（Google AdMob）
本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告（做完一輪題目後偶爾出現的全螢幕廣告，以及你**自行選擇**觀看以換取提示次數的獎勵廣告）。AdMob 可能會收集並處理：
- 裝置識別碼（例如廣告 ID、App set ID）
- 大略位置（由 IP 位址推估）
- 與廣告的互動（曝光、點擊）、App 啟動等使用資訊
- 診斷資訊（例如當機、效能資料）

這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，用於投放廣告、成效衡量與防詐騙。詳見 [Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)。
在歐洲經濟區、英國等地區，App 會先顯示同意畫面；你可以隨時在「設定 → 廣告隱私選項」修改。你也可以在 Android 系統設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID。

### 5. 通知
若你開啟「每日提醒」，App 會在本機排程通知。通知內容在你的手機上計算產生，不會傳送到任何伺服器。

### 5-1. 發音練習
「🔊 聽發音」使用手機內建的文字轉語音，在手機上產生語音。「🎤 跟著唸」會開啟手機系統的語音辨識服務（通常由 Google 提供），錄音由該服務處理、適用其隱私權政策；本 App 不會取得或保存錄音，只會收到辨識出的文字，用來和題目單字比對，比對完即丟棄。

### 5-2. 聯絡作者
「寄信給作者」會開啟你手機上的 Email App 並預填 App 版本、Android 版本與手機型號，你可以在寄出前修改或刪除。只有你按下傳送，我們才會收到你的 Email 地址與信件內容，僅用於回覆與改進 App，不會提供給第三方。

### 6. 如何刪除你的資料
- **手機上的資料**：設定 → 「清除這支手機上的學習資料」，或直接**移除 App**。
- **登出時**：可勾選「同時清除這支手機上的學習資料」。
- **Google Drive 上的備份**：設定 → 「刪除雲端備份」；或到 Google Drive 網頁版 → 設定 → 管理應用程式 → 找到本 App → 「刪除隱藏的應用程式資料」。
- **撤銷 Google 授權**：前往 [Google 帳戶 → 第三方應用程式與服務](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) 移除本 App。

### 7. 兒童隱私
本 App 的目標使用者為 13 歲以上，並非專為 13 歲以下兒童設計。廣告內容分級上限設定為「適合所有年齡（G）」。

## English

### 1. Our approach
English Practice ("the App") is offline-first. **The developer runs no servers or databases and never receives or sees your learning data.**

### 2. Data stored on your device
The following is stored only in the App's private storage on your device to provide App features:
- Answer history (question ID, correctness, time taken, mode)
- Wrong-answer notebook (wrong count, last wrong answer)
- Spaced-repetition schedule (next review dates, etc.)
- Daily streak records
- Settings such as hint credits and reminder time
- If you sign in with Google: your Google account email (only displayed in Settings as "Signed in as …")
- The public question bank and developer announcements downloaded from the internet (contain no personal data)

Android's automatic cloud backup is disabled for this App. **Uninstalling the App deletes all of the above from your device.**

### 3. Google Sign-In and Google Drive (optional)
- Signing in is **optional**. All learning features work without it, but your data will not carry over to another device.
- When you sign in, the App requests only the **`drive.appdata`** scope, which gives access to a hidden, App-specific folder in your Google Drive. **The App cannot read, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.**
- Purpose: back up the data in Section 2 (excluding your email) as a single file in **your own** Google Drive so you can restore it on a new device. The file counts toward your Drive storage, is transferred over HTTPS, and **never passes through the developer's servers**.
- Google Sign-In and Drive are provided by Google and governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App shows ads via Google AdMob (an occasional full-screen ad after finishing a set of questions, and rewarded ads that you **choose** to watch in exchange for hints). AdMob may collect and process:
- Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID, App set ID)
- Approximate location (derived from IP address)
- Ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and app usage such as app launches
- Diagnostics (e.g., crash and performance data)

Google processes this data under its own policies for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
In the EEA, UK and other applicable regions, a consent form is shown first; you can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings.

### 5. Notifications
If you enable daily reminders, notifications are scheduled locally. Their content is generated on your device and is not sent to any server.

### 5-1. Pronunciation practice
"🔊 Listen" uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech on the device. "🎤 Speak" opens your phone's system speech-recognition service (usually provided by Google), which processes the audio under its own privacy policy. The App never receives or stores audio; it only receives the recognized text, compares it with the target word, and discards it.

### 5-2. Contacting the developer
"Email the developer" opens your own email app with the App version, Android version and device model pre-filled; you can edit or remove them before sending. We only receive your email address and message if you choose to send it, and use them solely to reply and improve the App. We do not share them with third parties.

### 6. How to delete your data
- **On your device**: Settings → "Clear learning data on this phone", or simply **uninstall the App**.
- **When signing out**: check "Also clear learning data on this phone".
- **Google Drive backup**: Settings → "Delete cloud backup"; or in Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → this App → "Delete hidden app data".
- **Revoke Google access**: visit [Google Account → Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) and remove the App.

### 7. Children's privacy
The App is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. The maximum ad content rating is set to "G" (suitable for all audiences).

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date if this policy changes.

## 繁體中文（zh-TW）

**應用程式名稱**（30 字內）
```
英語練習：多益單字文法聽力
```

**簡短說明**（80 字內）
```
多益單字、文法、閱讀與聽力練習，錯題本＋間隔複習幫你記得更久，離線也能用。
```

**完整說明**（4000 字內）
```
專為多益與職場英文設計的練習 App，用零碎時間也能穩定進步。

【多益題型完整】
・單字：多益高頻字，附例句與易混淆字比較
・文法：Part 5 句子填空，每個錯誤選項都告訴你錯在哪
・閱讀：Part 6 段落填空、Part 7 email／廣告／公告閱讀
・聽力：Part 2 應答、Part 3 對話，男女聲朗讀，作答後才顯示原文

【記得更久】
・答錯自動收進錯題本，重做答對才移出
・SM-2 間隔重複演算法（類似 Anki）安排每題的複習日，首頁直接告訴你今天該複習幾題
・猜對的題目可以一鍵標記「其實是猜的」，加入收藏並安排明天再複習

【每天都想打開】
・每日練習：今日單字＋每日一題
・連續打卡紀錄
・和電腦對手 PK，比速度也比正確率
・每日提醒通知

【發音練習】
・單字一鍵朗讀，可切換慢速
・「跟著唸」用語音辨識檢查你的發音

【隱私優先】
・不需註冊，打開就能用；學習紀錄只存在你的手機
・想換手機保留進度？可選擇用 Google 登入，備份到「你自己的」Google Drive 隱藏資料夾，開發者看不到也碰不到
・題庫會自動更新，下載後離線也能作答

有任何建議或發現題目錯誤，歡迎在 App 內「意見回饋」來信。
```

## English (en-US)

**App name**
```
English Practice: TOEIC Prep
```

**Short description**
```
TOEIC vocabulary, grammar, reading & listening with spaced repetition. Works offline.
```

**Full description**
```
Practice English for the TOEIC and the workplace in just a few minutes a day.

TOEIC-STYLE QUESTIONS
• Vocabulary: high-frequency TOEIC words with examples and commonly confused words
• Grammar: Part 5 sentence completion — every wrong option is explained
• Reading: Part 6 text completion and Part 7 emails, ads and notices
• Listening: Part 2 responses and Part 3 conversations read aloud with two voices; the script appears after you answer

REMEMBER MORE
• Wrong answers go to your mistake notebook automatically
• SM-2 spaced repetition (like Anki) schedules every question; the home screen shows how many reviews are due today
• Got it right by guessing? Mark it and it comes back tomorrow

BUILD A HABIT
• Daily practice: word of the day + question of the day
• Study streaks and daily reminders
• Offline PK battles against computer opponents

PRIVACY FIRST
• No account required — your progress stays on your phone
• Optional Google sign-in backs up your progress to a hidden folder in YOUR OWN Google Drive; the developer can never access it
• The question bank updates automatically and works offline
```
